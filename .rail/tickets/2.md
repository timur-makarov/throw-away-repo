## Why
The calculator can't divide. Division by zero must give a clear error of the calculator's own, not a crash
from deep inside.

## Done Contract
- Check: `python3 -c "import calc; assert calc.div(6, 3) == 2"`
- Check: `python3 -c "import calc; exec('try:\n    calc.div(1, 0)\nexcept calc.CalcError:\n    pass\nelse:\n    raise SystemExit(1)')"`
- Check: `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -q`

## Out of scope
- Integer division and remainders.
