## Request
> Admins can delete a product but not a customer. Add delete_customer to the API, for admins only, with tests. A customer who has orders must not be deleted.

## Why
Admins can delete a product but have no way to remove a customer, so mistaken or obsolete customer records stay forever. Deleting must refuse a customer that still has orders, so order history never points at a missing customer.

## Done Contract
- Check: `python3 -m pytest -q tests/test_customers.py -k delete_customer`
- Check: `python3 -m pytest -q`
- Check: `ruff check .`

Behaviour: `api.delete_customer(request)` returns 403 unless `auth.allowed(request, "delete")` (checked before existence, like delete_product); 404 "no such customer" for a missing or non-int id; 409 with an error when any order in `store.list_orders()` has `customer_id` equal to the id, leaving the customer stored; otherwise 200 `{"status": 200}` and the customer is gone. `store.delete_customer(id)` is added in `shop/store.py` (shop/store.py is a person path, hence tier PERSON). Tests cover admin success, clerk/viewer 403 keeping the record, missing 404, bad id 404, and customer with orders 409.

## Out of scope
- shop/auth.py roles and actions do not change (the existing "delete" action is reused).
- No migration; stored data's shape does not change.
- Existing customer handlers, order handlers and the CSV export format do not change.

## Assumptions
- ASSUMED: orders reference customers by a `customer_id` field (seen in shop/export.py:19); orders without it belong to no customer.
- ASSUMED: 409 is the status for "customer has orders".
