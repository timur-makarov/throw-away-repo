## Why
Two reasons to bring the vendored engine up to the framework (timur-makarov/agentic-framework, `main`).

Sweep 6, findings F1 to F5 and F7: the guard's handling of other repositories fails open. A push to
`main` or a merge of this project's pull request passes the guard after `cd /tmp && cd -`, after `popd`,
inside a subshell, with `--git-dir`, or with `-R` naming this project from another directory, and the
`--owner` ask rule can be dodged by putting the flag first or abbreviating it. All are fixed in the framework.

The owner's cloud-first decision of 2026-11-12 (section 26 of the framework's plan). For this project it means:
ticket IDs come from one counter branch (`rail/ids`) instead of a branch per ID; a shipped ticket is moved by
whichever session looks, and work a session abandoned is resumed after an hour; `rail next` takes in issues
labelled `rail` by itself and labels waits at once; in a cloud session the guard no longer asks before
`sudo` and the like, and the three old ask rules leave `.claude/settings.json`; `.rail/ruleset.json` is
added for the owner to import on GitHub.

The work follows section 14 of the plan: diff the framework from the commit in `.rail/SOURCE`, read its
CHANGELOG, and copy the engine, the pack, the seed's settings rules and the ruleset in.
