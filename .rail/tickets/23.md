## Request
> shop/orders.py summary() gives the total but not how many lines the order has. Add a line count to the summary, with a test.

## Why
A person reading an order summary (api.get_order) sees the total but cannot tell how many lines make it up. A line count lets them check the total against the order at a glance.

## Done Contract
- Check: `python3 -m pytest -q tests/test_orders.py -k line_count`
- Check: `python3 -m pytest -q`

## Out of scope
- The shape of existing summary fields (id, total, status) does not change; the line count is an added key.
- No change to stored data, shop/store.py, shop/auth.py or migrations.

## Assumptions
- VERIFIED: summary() is only consumed by shop/api.py get_order, which passes it through unchanged (shop/api.py:9). Checked with grep for "summary(" in shop and tests: one caller, api.py:9, which returns it as "order" unchanged.
