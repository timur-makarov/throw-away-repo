## Request
> Each export in shop/export.py builds its header row on its own. Once there is a products export too, pull the header writing into one helper so the three cannot drift apart.

## Why
Each CSV export in shop/export.py (orders_csv, customers_csv, and the products export coming in ticket 22) builds its own writer and header row, so the exports can drift apart. One shared helper keeps header writing in a single place.

## Done Contract
- Check: `python3 -m pytest -q tests/test_export.py -k header_helper`
- Check: `python3 -m pytest -q tests/test_export.py`
- Check: `ruff check .`

## Out of scope
- The header names and row contents of orders_csv and customers_csv do not change; existing tests in tests/test_export.py still pass unchanged.
- The cell-safety rule (_safe) is not changed.
- shop/store.py, shop/auth.py, docs/api.md and stored data do not change.
- The products export itself belongs to ticket 22; this ticket covers the helper and the exports present on main.
